# Support

There is no guaranteed support, response time, or compatibility promise beyond the tests in this repository.

Questions can go to [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/eurafaeldecarvalho/tf-exclude/discussions). Bug reports and feature requests use the issue templates. A person may have at most 3 open pull requests at a time (see CONTRIBUTING.md).

tf-exclude runs your Terraform, with your credentials, on your machine. The maintainer does not operate it for you.
